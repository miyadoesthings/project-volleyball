This Privacy Policy explains how miya (“we”, “us”, “our”), the developer of Project Volleyball (the “Game”), collects, uses and shares information about the people who play it. It covers the Game on Meta Quest and the game servers and online services we operate for it.

The Game also relies on services run by other companies, including Meta and Unity. Their handling of your information is covered by their own privacy policies, which are linked in section 4.

If you have a question about this policy or your information, email us at miyaamiyamiyamiya@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information from your Meta account

With your permission, the Meta Platform provides us with your Meta user ID, your username (display name) and your profile picture. We use these to show your name in the Game and to recognise your account when you return.

1.2 Information the Game creates

  • A player ID issued by Unity Authentication. It is randomly generated and is not linked to your real name.
  • The name you use in the Game and the times you join matches.
  • The cosmetic items, effects and perks on your account, whether you earned them, received them from us or bought them.
  • Your player builds and settings, such as position, height, attributes and audio preferences. These are saved on your headset and are not sent to us.

1.3 Information collected during online play

  • Your IP address, which is needed to connect your headset to our servers.
  • Real-time gameplay data, including head and hand movement, ball contacts, team and score. We use this to run the match and do not keep it after the match ends.
  • Voice chat audio, if you have voice chat turned on. It is sent live to the other players in your match and is not recorded. You can turn voice chat or your microphone off at any time in the Game’s settings.

1.4 Purchases and subscriptions

The Game may offer in-app purchases and subscriptions through the Meta Quest Store. All payments are processed by Meta, and we never receive your card number or other payment details. Meta tells us which item or subscription you bought, when you bought it and whether a subscription is active, cancelled or expired. We keep that record with your Meta user ID and player ID so that we can deliver what you paid for and restore it if you reinstall the Game or change headsets.

1.5 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message.

We do not ask for your real name, and we do not collect your precise location, your contacts or your payment card information. The Game does not currently show advertising.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • run online matches, including showing your name, avatar and cosmetics to other players;
  • provide voice chat between players in the same match;
  • deliver and restore items, purchases and subscription features, and handle support and refund questions;
  • keep the Game fair and safe, which includes checking that player stats are valid, managing moderator access, and preventing cheating, fraud and abuse;
  • find and fix problems using our server logs;
  • reply to messages you send us; and
  • keep the records the law requires, such as records of purchases for tax and accounting.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or to build advertising profiles.

3. Legal bases for processing

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract, to provide the Game and anything you buy in it.
  • Legitimate interests, to keep the Game secure and fair, moderate player behaviour and fix problems.
  • Consent, for access to your Meta profile and for voice chat. You can withdraw consent by changing your Meta permissions or turning voice chat off.
  • Legal obligation, for records we are required to keep.

4. How information is shared

  • Other players in your match can see your in-game name, your avatar’s movements and the cosmetics you use, and can hear you if voice chat is on.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. provides account, entitlement and payment services. See Meta’s Privacy Policy at https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/
  • Unity Technologies provides player sign-in, the match list (Unity Lobby) and voice chat (Unity Vivox). See Unity’s Privacy Policy at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Vultr hosts our game servers, which process your IP address and gameplay data while you are connected.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our players or the integrity of the Game. If the Game is sold or transferred to a new owner, player information may be transferred as part of that transaction, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

5. How long we keep information

  • Match data and voice audio exist only while the match is running.
  • Account records (player ID, in-game name and match join times) are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.
  • Items and purchase records are kept for as long as they are on your account. After that, we keep purchase records only as long as we need them for refunds, fraud prevention, and tax and accounting requirements.
  • Server logs are rotated automatically and deleted after a few days.
  • Emails are kept until your question or request has been dealt with.
  • Builds and settings stay on your headset until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can turn voice chat or your microphone off in the Game’s settings.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time through your Meta account. Refunds are handled by Meta under its refund policy.
  • Uninstalling the Game removes everything the Game has stored on your headset.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing miyaamiyamiyamiya@gmail.com with your in-game name. We will reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. You do not need to delete your Meta account to make a request.
  • Deleting our records removes items that exist only on our servers. Anything you bought through Meta stays attached to your Meta account and can be restored.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as the right to object to or restrict certain processing, the right to data portability, or the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, contact us and we will delete any records we hold about them.

8. International transfers

Our game servers are located in the United States. Meta and Unity process information in several countries. If you play from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws may differ from those where you live.

9. Security

Information travels over the internet between your headset, our servers, and Meta and Unity. We limit access to our servers to authorised administrators and keep only the information described in this policy. No online service can guarantee complete security, however.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before the Game starts collecting new types of information or using information in new ways, for example when new online features or new purchase options are added. When we do, we will change the effective date above, and we will announce significant changes in the Game or in its release notes before they take effect.
